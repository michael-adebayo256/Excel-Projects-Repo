## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed during my **Level 3 Data Technician Bootcamp with Leep Talent**.

I used Microsoft Excel to clean, organise, analyse and visualise retail and sales datasets. The project focused on developing practical spreadsheet skills and transforming raw data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🛠️ Skills & Techniques

### 🧮 Excel Formulas

| Function | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| `SUM()` | Calculate total sales and commissions |
| `SUMIF()` | Calculate totals based on conditions |
| `AVERAGE()` | Calculate average sales and commissions |
| `AVERAGEIF()` | Calculate conditional averages |
| `DATE()` | Create and manage dates |
| `MONTH()` | Extract months from dates |
| `YEAR()` | Extract years for reporting |
| `UNIQUE()` | Identify distinct values |
| `VLOOKUP()` | Retrieve matching data |
| `SWITCH()` | Classify sales into performance categories |

## 📂 Datasets Analysed

### 🛍️ Retail Sales Analysis
Analysed customer transactions, product categories, sales revenue and commissions.

### 🚴 Bike Sales Analysis
Used PivotTables to compare sales by country, age group and gender.

### 💻 Regional Product Sales
Analysed laptop, smartphone and printer sales across English counties using PivotTables and the `SWITCH` function.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- 📈 Adults aged 35–64 recorded the highest bike sales order quantity.
- 🌎 The United States and Australia were among the strongest markets.
- 👩 Female customers recorded higher total order quantities than male customers.
- 🛒 Retail sales and commissions were summarised using Excel formulas.
- 🎯 Product sales were classified into **High**, **Medium** and **Low** categories.
