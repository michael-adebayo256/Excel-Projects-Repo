# Excel Data Analysis | Data Technician Bootcamp

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Level 3 Data Technician Bootcamp with Leep Talent**. It involved using Microsoft Excel to organise, analyse and visualise retail and sales datasets, applying spreadsheet techniques to identify trends, calculate performance metrics and present useful business insights.

## Skills and Techniques

- **Excel Formulas:** Used `SUM`, `SUMIF`, `AVERAGE` and `AVERAGEIF` to calculate sales totals, commissions, averages and conditional summaries.
- **Date Functions:** Worked with `DATE`, `MONTH` and `YEAR` to manage dates and support time-based analysis.
- **Data Lookup:** Used `VLOOKUP` to retrieve information and `UNIQUE` to identify distinct values.
- **Data Cleaning and Organisation:** Applied filtering and sorting to organise customer records, transactions and product information.
- **PivotTables:** Summarised sales performance by product category, county, country, age group and gender.
- **SWITCH Function:** Categorised product sales volumes into High, Medium and Low performance groups.
- **Data Visualisation:** Created Excel charts to compare sales performance and highlight trends across different customer segments and markets.

## Datasets Analysed

The project involved three main datasets:

1. **Retail Sales:** Analysed transactions, product categories, customer demographics, sales revenue and commissions.
2. **Bike Sales:** Used PivotTables and charts to compare sales across countries, age groups and genders.
3. **Regional Product Sales:** Analysed sales volumes for laptops, smartphones and printers across English counties.

## Key Findings

- Identified differences in sales performance across customer age groups and genders.
- Used PivotTables to compare sales activity across international markets.
- Calculated retail sales totals and commission values using Excel formulas.
- Categorised regional product sales using the `SWITCH` function.
- Presented findings through charts and summary tables to support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel | Excel Formulas | PivotTables | Data Filtering | Sorting | Charts | Data Analysis**

## Learning Outcomes

This project strengthened my practical Excel skills in data preparation, analysis, reporting and visualisation. It also helped me develop confidence in turning raw business data into structured information that can support business decisions.
